## Results

### 1. InstantID

**Package**: `ComfyUI_InstantID` by cubiq
**Installation Status**: [x] Installed
**Workflow Complexity**: [x] Complex
**Setup Notes**: Requires InstantID IP-Adapter model, ControlNet model, and InsightFace (antelopev2)

**Dependencies**:
- [x] InsightFace (antelopev2) installed
- [x] onnxruntime / onnxruntime-gpu installed
- [x] Works with SDXL only

**Workflow File**: `portrait_instantid.json`

**Key Findings**:

**Reference Image Type Matters:**
- **Pixel art reference** → Better face consistency, but output style degraded (less appealing)
- **Clean non-pixel-art reference** → Better pixel art style output, but weaker face consistency

**Strength Trade-off Problem:**
- **High strength (0.6-0.8)**: Strong face lock, but kills pixel art style + blocks expressions
- **Low strength (0.1-0.2)**: Preserves pixel art style + allows expressions, but weak face consistency
- **No sweet spot found**: Can't get strong consistency without sacrificing style/expressions

**Final Configuration Tested**:
- Reference: Clean non-pixel-art portrait (no pixel quantization)
- Workflow: CheckpointLoader → LoraLoader (pixel art) → ApplyInstantID → KSampler
- InstantID weight: 0.2
- LoRA applied BEFORE InstantID (after InstantID caused validation errors)

**Test Results:**
- Face Consistency (1-10): **4/10**
  - General face shape maintained
  - **Critical drift observed**: Freckle placement/color changes, hair color variations (green tips appearing/disappearing), sweater pattern inconsistent
  - Details not locked in despite recognizable face
- Style Preservation (1-10): **8/10**
  - Pixel art style intact at low strength (0.2)
  - Style degraded at higher strengths
- Expression Control (1-10): **7/10**
  - Works at low strength (0.1-0.2)
  - "Angry" and "happy" expressions worked
  - Many subtle expressions had little to no effect
- Quality Issues:
  - [x] Detail drift (freckles, hair color, outfit patterns)
  - [x] Limited expression range at low strength
  - [x] Style degradation at high strength
  - [ ] No distorted features, extra limbs, or artifacts
- VRAM Usage: [x] Medium

**Sample Images:**
- Angry: Generated (looked similar to base, but details drifted)
- Other expressions: Not completed due to fundamental issues

**Overall Notes**:

**CRITICAL FAILURE FOR PROJECT REQUIREMENTS:**

InstantID requires very low strength (0.1-0.2) to preserve pixel art style, but at this strength it cannot maintain consistent character details across generations. Observed drift in:
- Freckle placement and color
- Hair color (green tips appearing/disappearing)
- Outfit details (sweater pattern)

**Verdict**: Not suitable for multi-generation RP sessions (50+ images) due to detail drift. The strength required for pixel art preservation is too weak to lock in character consistency.

**Architecture Issue**: InstantID + heavy stylization (pixel art LoRA) creates an unsolvable trade-off between style preservation and face consistency.
